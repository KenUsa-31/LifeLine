# Lifeline reference notes

Lifeline is an educational prototype for a fictional dialysis-center companion. The sources below inform the reviewed knowledge base. They do not verify a real center, its staff, services, schedule, prices, or contact information.

- National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (NIDDK), [Hemodialysis](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/kidney-disease/kidney-failure/hemodialysis) — treatment basics, access, food/fluid guidance, adjustment to treatment routines, fatigue after in-center treatment, and coping support.
- NHS, [Complications of dialysis](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/dialysis/complications/) — signs that warrant urgent medical advice and fistula bleeding emergency guidance.
- World Health Organization, [Emergency care](https://www.who.int/health-topics/emergency-care) — general emergency-care context.

Content reviewed for this prototype on 2026-11-14. Have a qualified local nephrology clinician review all clinical content and emergency wording before real-world use.
